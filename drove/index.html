About DROVE & the model

DROVE animates USAMM model projections — it is not live tracking. Every flow you see is the annual mean of modeled shipments from the U.S. Animal Movement Model (Colorado State University): the mean of 1,000 modeled networks for cattle, beef and dairy, and 250 for swine. The unit is modeled shipments, not USDA/NASS counts.

How the animation encodes data

  • Dot rate on each link is proportional to that partner's modeled shipments/yr through one shared, automatically chosen scale — the legend chip always states the live "1 dot ≈ K shipments/yr", and rescaling never changes the proportions between links. One full dot journey ≈ one modeled year.
  • Ribbon width ∝ √(shipments/yr). Partners under 1% of out-of-state flow keep their ribbon but emit no dots (stated in the legend when active).
  • Dot size (cattle family, outgoing view only) reflects typical shipment size — head, estimated from shipment-size-bin midpoints. Swine dots are uniform (swine reports shipments only — no head estimate; its saved bins are cattle-scaled), and inbound dots are uniform too: the pipeline reports no inbound shipment-size distribution, and DROVE never reuses outbound provenance for the inbound view.
  • Dot color in the Cattle view (outgoing only) samples the state's real beef/dairy commodity split — warmer green = beef share, teal = dairy share, in the state's actual proportions. Inbound cattle is combined-commodity in the model, so inbound dots stay one color.
  • ▶ Year cycle replays the modeled year on a loop: emission follows each quarter's real share of the state total (the same four numbers as the Q1–Q4 buttons — smoothly blended between quarters for display, never quoted as sub-quarterly data).
  • The orbit ring around the hub shows the in-state share: each orbiting dot ≈ 1% of total shipments that stay in-state (outbound) or arrive from within the state (inbound) — a share indicator, deliberately on a different scale than the flow dots (both scales are stated here). Inbound totals include in-state arrivals; the halo counts only the out-of-state remainder.
  • The outer halo absorbs the residual: out-of-state flow beyond the 12 ranked partners, labeled "other states (combined)" — so what you see totals honestly. The faint dust wandering between the hub and the halo ring is the drift — that residual flow in motion, deliberately dimmed so the ranked streams stay the story. It is data (its rate uses the same "1 dot ≈ K" scale), just unranked.
  • The thin rail on the right edge is a playback-speed control (0.25×–3×). It changes only how fast the modeled year replays — every proportion, the dot scale, and the "one loop ≈ one modeled year" mapping are unchanged (a faster loop is still one modeled year). The live legend states the rate whenever it isn't 1×.
  • The hub's slow breathing and shimmer are ambient motion — they encode no data. The orbit's spin direction is a convention only: clockwise for outgoing, counter-clockwise for inbound. Arcs are schematic connectors between state centroids, not transport routes. Dragging a finger (or moving the cursor) through the streams makes nearby dots shimmy around it — pure play, purely visual: their route, timing, size and color are untouched.

Directions & seasons

  • Inbound (cattle & swine) uses the model's own destination-side ranked partner arrays — never relabeled outbound data. A state's inbound picture is assembled from other states' ICVI fits, so it inherits their data vintage and coverage (slightly softer than outbound). Beef/dairy have no inbound facts, so their inbound animation is disabled — the county lens offers their real static inbound maps instead.
  • Season uses each partner corridor's own real quarterly breakdown (baked 2026-08-14 from the raw networks by generate_flow_detail.R) — partners genuinely surge at different times of the modeled year. Corridors below the bake's 1-shipment/yr floor fall back to the state-level share (the caption says so). The outer "other states" halo stays at its annual mean — residual quarters are not broken out. Inbound quarters come from the same per-pair bake, aggregated for the season bars.
  • Stream steadiness (cattle & swine): each corridor's emission regularity reflects its across-network consistency — steady corridors flow like metronomes, corridors the model is uncertain about visibly sputter. The modulation is mean-preserving (long-run rate stays ∝ the modeled mean) and derives from the real standard deviation across the 1,000 / 250 networks. Tap any ribbon to see the middle-80% range behind it.

What is not here

  • Inventory is not modeled. Nodes pulse and decay — nothing accumulates or holds a stock level, anywhere.
  • County-level flows are not animated — the facts are state-level. The county lens shows the real generated county choropleths (the same maps as the printed flyers) as the sole county-level authority.
  • Sparse swine states (Montana, New Mexico, New York — fewer than 750 modeled outgoing swine shipments/yr) suppress all swine views, matching the flyers. Swine vintage: 2010–2011 ICVI.

Companion app: Pocket USAMM (reference cards, full honesty statement). Authoritative methods: USAMM_figures_methods.pdf in the flyer package.
